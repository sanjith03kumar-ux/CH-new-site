Round Tour of Sri Lanka

The classic loop: rock fortress, sacred Kandy, tea country and the south coast.

Culture & HeritageHills & Sky

Duration: 8 days

The classic loop: rock fortress, sacred Kandy, tea country and the south coast. Every Crystal Holidays tour is tailor-made: dates, hotels, pace and transport can all be adjusted.

Every trip is tailor-made, so the rate depends on your dates, hotels and group size. Send a booking request and our team will email you the rate and full details.

Send a booking request Ask on WhatsApp

Highlights

  • Sigiriya rock fortress
  • Temple of the Tooth, Kandy
  • Tea estates around Nuwara Eliya
  • Galle Fort and the south coast

Sample itinerary

  1. Day 1: Airport to Negombo
  2. Day 2: Sigiriya and Dambulla
  3. Day 3: Polonnaruwa, then Kandy
  4. Day 4: Kandy temple and cultural show
  5. Day 5: Tea country, Nuwara Eliya
  6. Day 6: Safari and drive south
  7. Day 7: Galle Fort, beach time
  8. Day 8: Colombo and departure

This is a starting point. We can change the route, hotels and pace to suit you.

What is included

  • Airport pick-up and drop-off
  • Hotel accommodation (to be confirmed with you)
  • Chauffeur-driven air-conditioned vehicle
  • English-speaking driver or guide
  • 24-hour assistance
