Questions we often hear

Quick answers before you plan your trip.

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

There is a good coast somewhere all year. The south and west coasts and hill country are best from about December to March, while the east coast is at its best from about April to September. Tell us your month and we will plan around the weather.

Can you change a tour to suit us?

Yes. Every itinerary is a starting point. We can change hotels, dates, pace and activities, or build a trip from scratch.

Do you arrange the Maldives too?

Yes. Many guests combine a Sri Lanka tour with a few days at a Maldives resort, and we arrange both together.

How do I get a rate and book?

Open any tour and send a booking request, or use the inquiry form. We email you the rate and full details. Nothing is paid until you are happy to confirm.

How do I pay?

After you confirm, you pay a deposit by bank transfer. Ask our AI assistant for the payment details whenever you are ready.

Do I need a visa to visit Sri Lanka?

Most visitors need an electronic travel authorisation or visa before arrival, and the rules can change. Please check the official Sri Lanka immigration website for your nationality, and we are happy to help.

Is Sri Lanka safe for families and solo travellers?

Sri Lanka is welcoming to visitors, and many families and solo travellers enjoy it. We follow official travel advice and look after you throughout your trip.

Ready to plan your trip?

Tell us your dates, group size and interests. We reply within one working day, and no payment is needed to send a request.
