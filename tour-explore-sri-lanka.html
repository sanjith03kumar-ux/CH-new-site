Explore Sri Lanka

Our longest all-round tour: ancient cities, hills, wildlife and beach.

Our longest all-round tour: ancient cities, hills, wildlife and beach. Every Crystal Holidays tour is tailor-made: dates, hotels, pace and transport can all be adjusted.

Highlights

  • Ancient cities of the cultural triangle
  • Tea country by train
  • Yala or Udawalawe safari
  • Beach days on the south coast

Sample itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive Colombo
  2. Day 2-3: Anuradhapura and Sigiriya
  3. Day 4: Polonnaruwa
  4. Day 5-6: Kandy
  5. Day 7: Nuwara Eliya and tea
  6. Day 8: Ella by train
  7. Day 9: Safari
  8. Day 10-11: South coast beach
  9. Day 12: Departure

This is a starting point. We can change the route, hotels and pace to suit you.

What is included

  • Hotel accommodation (to be confirmed with you)
  • Chauffeur-driven air-conditioned vehicle

  • 24-hour assistance
