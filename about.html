Three decades of showing Sri Lanka properly

A local team that plans every journey around you.

Crystal Holidays is a destination management company serving visitors from the USA, Europe, the Far East, South Asia, Australia and the Maldives. We plan every detail: itinerary, hotels, chauffeur-driven or rental transport, and bilingual guides.

Our Managing Director, Mr. Wansa Hettiarachchi, has more than 30 years in travel and tourism. We also own Crystal Villas, a five-room private boutique villa in Weligama, and Westend Hotels in Colombo, known as GSH City Hotel. Our hospitality portfolio also includes MAMA ROMA, an authentic Italian restaurant in Weligama, and our new Mount Lavinia outlet: MAMA ROMA Italian Restaurant, Cafe & Sports Bar, with 150 covers. Visit mamaromasrilanka.com or mamaromaitalian.com.
